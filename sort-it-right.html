Sort It Right

Sell it

  • PET bottles (water, soda)
  • HDPE bottles (shampoo, detergent)
  • Clean basins, pails and crates
  • Plastic chairs without metal parts

Prepare it

  1. Empty and rinse
  2. Remove caps and labels if you can
  3. Crush or flatten to save space
  4. Keep it dry and bagged by type

Ask first

  • Candy wrappers and sachets
  • Styrofoam food boxes
  • Greasy takeout containers

Many junk shops won't buy these. Call the shop first.

MYTH VS FACT

Myth: junk shops only take huge loads.

Fact: many shops buy by the kilo, even a small sack. Check the Price Board and call ahead.
