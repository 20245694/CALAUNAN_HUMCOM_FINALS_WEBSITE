Hazardous Waste Drop-off

Do not sell or bin these items. They can leak toxic chemicals or start fires. Take them to a drop-off point.

Batteries

Tape the terminals of used dry-cell and lithium batteries and keep them in a dry container.

Drop-off: Barangay Pacdal covered court, 1st Saturday, 8:00 AM – 12:00 NN

E-waste

Old phones, chargers and radios. Wipe personal data and keep the item whole.

Drop-off: Baguio City Materials Recovery Facility, Mon–Fri, 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM

Used cooking oil

Let it cool, pour into a sealed bottle and never down the drain.

Drop-off: Brgy. Aurora Hill hall, every Friday, 2:00 – 5:00 PM

Light bulbs

Wrap fluorescent and CFL bulbs in newspaper so they do not break.

Drop-off: Tatay Berto Scrap & Bottles, Mon–Sat, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
